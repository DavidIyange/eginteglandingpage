Every change leaves a record. The value is in reading it in time.

A new admin account, a changed permission or a burst of failed sign-ins looks small on its own. An audit trail puts those events in one place, in order, so someone can act on them.

Questions we are often asked

Do we need both products?

Not always. Netwrix Auditor gives you detailed visibility of changes and access. Microsoft Sentinel gives you wider threat monitoring. Many organisations use both, and we advise on the right starting point after a short assessment.

Can this help us meet NDPA requirements?

These tools help you see who accesses personal data, detect misuse and produce evidence. Full compliance also depends on your policies and processes, and we can advise on that as part of the engagement.

Does Microsoft Sentinel only work if we use Microsoft products?

Sentinel runs on Microsoft Azure and fits best in Microsoft-centred environments, but it can also collect data from other sources through connectors. Its cost depends on how much data you send to it, so we size this with you before you commit.

What will it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your environment, such as the number of users and systems. After a short call we give you a written quote with no obligation.

Do you help after installation?

Yes. We help with deployment and training, and we agree support terms with you in the quote.
